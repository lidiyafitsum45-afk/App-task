How Priority works

The four boxes. Every task lives in one of four quadrants based on whether it's Important and/or Urgent. Pick the box directly when you create a task — there's no auto-guessing.

Moving a task. Drag a task card into a different quadrant to re-file it (desktop). On mobile, open the task and use the "Move to…" buttons.

Tags. Shared across the whole team — pick from the library or add a new one with your own icon.

Notes & attachments. Notes are free-form details. Attachments are labelled links (specs, docs, Figma files, anything with a URL) — no file uploads, just links.

Subtasks. Full mini-tasks with their own assignee and due date, nested under a parent task. They don't show up on the main dashboard — only inside their parent.

Process chains. An ordered sequence of steps, possibly across different people. Only the active step is visible; finishing it activates the next one automatically.

Done tasks. Completed tasks disappear from the four boxes. Tap "✓ Done" below the top bar to see them.

My progress. Tracks your own completions — a heatmap of which days you got things done, and how your completions split across the four boxes.

Notifications. Tap 🔔 once to allow push notifications for assignments, chain hand-offs, and due/overdue reminders — works even with the app closed.
